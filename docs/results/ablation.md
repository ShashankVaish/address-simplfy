## Calibration and reliability

Generated by `eval/reliability.py` on the **dev** split, stack **R2**, local
provider (150 addresses). "Correct" means every extracted field matches the
gold and the geocode is within 500 m.

![Reliability diagram](./reliability.svg)

| | ECE |
|---|---|
| Raw confidence score | 0.217 |
| Isotonic-calibrated, in-sample | 0.000 |
| Isotonic-calibrated, 2-fold cross-validated | **0.057** |

The cross-validated number is the honest one: an isotonic fit on 150 points
is coarse (8 knots), and the in-sample 0.000 is what any monotone fit
achieves on its own training data. The fitted map ships with the layer as
`data/calibration.json` and is applied in S6.

**Threshold at 95% precision:** none found on this split with ≥ 10 supporting
addresses. Without a model the pipeline rarely gets both the fields *and* the
geocode right, so no confidence band is precise enough to auto-resolve at the
target. This is what makes the ablation rows B–E the decisive ones: the
threshold and the auto-resolution rate are re-fitted on stack E in the cloud.

## Learning curve

Generated by `eval/learning_curve.py`: an **empty** landmark graph is fed the
corpus one address at a time (600 addresses, 36 localities), each address is
resolved against the graph as it stands, and then its delivery is "confirmed"
with the corpus's own coordinates — what the learner Lambda does on a real
`DeliveryConfirmed` event.

![Learning curve](./learning_curve.svg)

| Addresses already seen in the locality | n | Geocoded from the graph | Median geocode error |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 36 | 5.6% | 1,221 m |
| 1 | 36 | **80.6%** | **0 m** |
| 2–3 | 72 | 76.4% | 0 m |
| 4–7 | 141 | 80.9% | 0 m |
| 8–15 | 280 | 76.4% | 0 m |
| 16+ | 35 | 74.3% | 0 m |

The curve is a step, not a slope: one confirmed delivery into a locality
takes the graph from a pincode-centroid guess (1.2 km) to the place itself.
The remaining ~20% that never hit the graph are addresses whose wording
shares no landmark with an earlier one; those still fall through to the
geocoder or centroid. The "confirmation" here uses ground truth, not real
riders, so this shows the *mechanism* transfers across differently-worded
addresses, not field performance.
